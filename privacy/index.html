Privacy policy

Last updated: November 2, 2026

Short version: this website doesn’t track you. We count anonymous page visits, with no cookies and nothing that identifies you. We only have your information if you email us or, once template sales open, buy something.

This policy covers knadigitalstudio.com. Our apps have their own policies, such as the Wrap It Up privacy policy.

What this website collects

Very little. There are no accounts, no cookies, no ads and no tracking across other sites. We use Cloudflare Web Analytics to count page visits in total: which pages are read, which site sent the visit, and the country, browser and device type. It uses no cookies, doesn’t follow you across websites and doesn’t identify you. We only ever see totals, never individual visitors.

The site is hosted on GitHub Pages. Like any web host, GitHub may record technical information such as your IP address to keep the service secure. That is covered by GitHub’s privacy statement; we don’t see or use it.

When you join our email list or email us

If you sign up for our emails, your address is stored by MailerLite, the service we use to send them. You confirm by email before you’re added, and we only send what you signed up for. We’ve turned off open tracking. Every email has an unsubscribe link.

If you email us directly, we keep your address and message so we can reply. We never sell or share your information. Ask us to delete it anytime and we will.

When you buy a template

Template sales aren’t open yet. When they are, payment will be handled by a checkout provider that acts as the seller of record. They process your payment and taxes; we never see your card details. We receive your name, email address and order details so we can deliver the files and support you.

Children

This website isn’t aimed at children under 13, and we don’t knowingly collect their information.

Changes

If this policy changes, we’ll update this page and the date at the top.
