Privacy policy

Last updated: October 26, 2026

Short version: this website doesn’t track you. We only have your information if you email us or, once template sales open, buy something.

This policy covers knadigitalstudio.com. Our apps have their own policies, such as the Wrap It Up privacy policy.

What this website collects

Nothing on its own. There are no accounts, no cookies, no analytics, no ads and no tracking scripts.

The site is hosted on GitHub Pages. Like any web host, GitHub may record technical information such as your IP address to keep the service secure. That is covered by GitHub’s privacy statement; we don’t see or use it.

When you email us

If you email us, including to join a release list, we keep your email address and message so we can reply or send the updates you asked for. We never sell or share it. Ask us to delete it anytime and we will.

When you buy a template

Template sales aren’t open yet. When they are, payment will be handled by a checkout provider that acts as the seller of record. They process your payment and taxes; we never see your card details. We receive your name, email address and order details so we can deliver the files and support you.

Children

This website isn’t aimed at children under 13, and we don’t knowingly collect their information.

Changes

If this policy changes, we’ll update this page and the date at the top.

Contact

Questions or deletion requests: knadigitalstudio@gmail.com
