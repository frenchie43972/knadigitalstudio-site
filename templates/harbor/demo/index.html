Questions

Good to know before you call

Do you charge for estimates?
No. Estimates are free. For repairs we charge a flat service-call fee, which goes toward the job if you go ahead.
Are your technicians licensed and insured?
Yes. Every plumber and electrician is state licensed, and we carry full liability insurance. We’re happy to show proof.
Do you really answer at night and on weekends?
Yes. Emergency calls go to a person on our team, 24 hours a day, 7 days a week, including holidays.
How do I pay?
Card, check or bank transfer when the job is done. Financing is available for larger projects.

Need it fixed today?

Call for same-day service, or send a quick request and we’ll reply within the hour.
