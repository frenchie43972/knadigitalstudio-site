What we do

Four ways we feed our neighbors

From a weekly grocery shop to a box at the door, every program is free and open to all.

Food pantry

Free groceries every week. Choose what your family needs, like a small grocery store.

Learn more about Food pantry

Community meals

A hot dinner every Wednesday, open to everyone. Come hungry, stay for the company.

Learn more about Community meals

Summer lunches for kids

Free lunches at parks and libraries when school is out. Any child 18 or under can eat.

Learn more about Summer lunches for kids

Grocery delivery for seniors

Weekly groceries brought to the door for neighbors who can’t easily get out.

Learn more about Grocery delivery for seniors
