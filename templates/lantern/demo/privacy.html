Privacy policy

Last updated: [date]. This is a starting point. Have it reviewed for your organization and location.

What we collect

When you get food, volunteer, donate, sign up for our newsletter or contact us, we collect the details you give us, such as your name, address, email, phone number and household size.

How we use it

Only to provide our programs, arrange volunteering, process and receipt donations, send the newsletter you asked for, and report anonymous totals to our funders. We don’t sell or rent your information.

Donations

Online gifts are handled by our donation provider. We never see or store your full card number.

Who we share it with

Only with services that help us run the organization, such as our donation, email and form providers, and only as needed for those services. Information about the people we serve is never shared.

How long we keep it

As long as we need it for our programs and records, or as required by law.

Your choices

You can ask to see, correct or delete your information, or unsubscribe from emails, at any time by writing to hello@example.com.
