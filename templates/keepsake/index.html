A closer look

Questions

Why not use a free wedding site?
Free sites from registry companies come with their branding, ads or upsells to your guests, and look like everyone else’s. Keepsake is yours: your own web address, your design, nothing selling to your guests, and you can keep it online for as long as you like.
How do RSVPs reach us?
The RSVP form connects to a free form service (the guide shows Formspree, Netlify Forms, Tally and Google Forms; on WordPress, a free form plugin). Replies arrive by email, and most services let you download them as a spreadsheet.
Can we keep it private?
Yes. It asks search engines not to list it from the start, and the guide shows how to add a password with common hosts or in WordPress.
Do we need to know how to code?
No. The HTML guide walks you through changing names, dates, photos and colors with a free text editor. Prefer pointing and clicking? Choose the WordPress version.
Can a wedding planner or photographer use it for clients?
Yes. Each purchase covers one website, so buy once per couple. See the license.
Are the demo photos included?
No. The demo uses free stock photos for preview only. The download has placeholders for your own photos.
