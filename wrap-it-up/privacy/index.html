Privacy Policy for Wrap It Up

Effective date: October 16, 2026

This policy explains how the Wrap It Up app (“the app”), made by K&A Digital Studio (“we”, “us”), handles information. The short version: we don’t collect any personal information.

Information we collect

None. The app has no accounts or sign-in, and it doesn’t use analytics, advertising, or tracking. It does not send any data to us or to any server we run.

Information stored on your device

The app saves a few settings on your own device so it remembers your choices between uses:

  • Your selected phrase
  • Sound, volume, vibration, and silent-mode settings
  • Whether you’ve seen the disclaimer
  • Which in-app purchases you own, so they work offline

This information stays on your device. We can’t see it. Uninstalling the app or clearing its data deletes it.

In-app purchases

Purchases are handled entirely by Google Play. We never receive your payment details. Google Play tells the app which items you own so it can unlock them. Google’s handling of your information is covered by the Google Privacy Policy.

Permissions

The app uses your phone’s vibration feature for optional haptic feedback. It does not access your contacts, location, camera, microphone, or files.

Children

The app is not directed at children under 13, and we do not knowingly collect information from anyone, including children.

Changes to this policy

If the app changes in a way that affects your privacy, for example by adding new features, we will update this page and change the effective date above.

Contact

Questions about this policy? Email knadigitalstudio@gmail.com.
