Wrap It Up

Someone talking way too long? Hold up your phone, press the big button, and a full-screen phrase does the talking for you.

What it does

  • One big button. One tap. A bold, pulsing message like “WRAP IT UP!” fills the screen.
  • Pick from a set of phrases, from polite nudges to pure snark.
  • Optional sound effect and vibration, with a one-tap silent mode.
  • Works in portrait and landscape.

Free, with optional extras

Wrap It Up is free to download. Extra phrase packs are available as small one-time purchases. No subscriptions.

Novelty use only

It’s for laughs. Point it at people responsibly.
