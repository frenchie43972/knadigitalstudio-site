About

Hi, I’m Kris.

I run K&A Digital Studio, a one-person studio making small, useful things: apps, website templates and printables for the everyday needs bigger products skip.

Get in touch See what I make

Why I started K&A

I kept running into the same thing: a small, real problem with no simple tool for it, or a tool buried under ads, sign-ups and subscriptions. So I started building the simple version myself, making sure people can find it, and pricing it fairly.

How I work

  • Real problems first. Every product starts with a need I’ve seen, usually one that looked “too small” for bigger companies.
  • No ads, no tracking. My apps and templates don’t carry ads or follow you around. You pay once, or not at all.
  • A real person answers. Every email to the studio reaches me, and I reply myself.

What I make

  • Small apps: simple tools that do one job well, with no ads and no sign-ups.
  • Website templates: professional sites without a developer bill. Harbor for local businesses, Lantern for nonprofits, Keepsake for weddings.
  • TidyHarbor: my Etsy shop of printables for busy households, from holiday games to budgets and planners.
