About

An independent studio that builds things by hand

KNA Digital Studio started with one app, Wrap It Up, a single big button for ending a conversation. It grew into a small studio that makes apps and website templates.

We're independent, with no ad network to answer to. That means we can build things the slow, careful way and keep them small enough to understand every line.

The rule for everything we ship is the same: write it by hand, keep it light, and never track the people who use it. That's why our templates have no page builders or frameworks, and why our apps have no ads.

What's on the bench

Wrap It Up is in its Android beta ahead of a Google Play launch. Our first website templates, starting with Harbor for local service businesses, are in the works. Get notified when they ship.
